## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Container instantiate
A container child given a row SHALL take the row's item through the object database's dispatcher, with flags 0. When the result's record has an item body, the palette SHALL be rolled on the row's wealth, the count byte SHALL take the wealth less its count as a signed fraction, damage and dirt the row's shares of 255 with their spreads, and the apparel walker SHALL run; otherwise a world or local copy with a weapon section SHALL take damage and dirt and their spreads and the weapon walker SHALL run.

#### Scenario: An apparel and a weapon picked
- **WHEN** a chest's two children name tables of an instance-on-use apparel and weapon with damage 0.5, spread 0.25, dirt 0.75, spread 0.5
- **THEN** the children name two new local copies; the weapon copy holds damage 128 and dirt 191 with those spreads, and the apparel copy those spreads
