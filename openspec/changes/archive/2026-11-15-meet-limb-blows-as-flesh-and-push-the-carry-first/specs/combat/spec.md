## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A blow on a limb meets flesh
The hit reaction SHALL choose the parry, the clash and the block by what the blade touched: a struck weapon clashes, a struck shield blocks, and a struck limb SHALL be met as flesh whatever the struck fighter's guard, so a parrying fighter's parry answers only a blow on its parrying weapon.

#### Scenario: A limb struck through a parry
- **WHEN** a parrying fighter and an open one are cut on the same limb by the same blade
- **THEN** both lose the same red and yellow health, the striker's swing flags are the same, and the parrying fighter's parry stays up
