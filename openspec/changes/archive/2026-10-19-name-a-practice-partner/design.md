# Design

## Ladder

`next_practice` advances the fight count by one and stops at 8. The name is `opponent` plus two digits. Novice uses the count. Expert adds 8. Count 8 is the last fight. Red becomes the float of the double maximum times 0.1 plus the double red, then the maximum when that float is greater. Yellow copies those bits. A non-number is not replaced by the maximum.
