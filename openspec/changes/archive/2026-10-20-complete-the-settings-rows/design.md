# Design

## Behaviour

The Settings list has a Music toggle at row 2, column 1, next to the audio slider. The Controls list has 22 rows; the first 14 are the screenshot's visible page, and the remaining eight are the template's lower rows: Skills/K, Journal/J, Talk/T, then Use Power, Power Bar, Previous Power, Next Power and Power Tree with no default key text.

## Model

`exa_ui::settings` adds the rows and updates the counts.
