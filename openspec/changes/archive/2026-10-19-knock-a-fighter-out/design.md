# Design

## Knock-out

`see_target` calls `knock_out` with delay 0. The wake clock is the wrapping sum of the clock and the delay. Flags are masked with 0xffffff0f and then gain 0x30. The condition is down, the statue mark clears, gait is 0, tone is 0, and the pin gain is the float whose bits are 0x3da3d70a. The body countdown rises to 256 only when it is lower. A blow does not call it. Part damage multipliers stay the bone table. Health does not write a per-limb impairment.

## Open questions

- No stored limb impairment was found. Low health does not slow a limb.
- The damage path does not knock a fighter out.
