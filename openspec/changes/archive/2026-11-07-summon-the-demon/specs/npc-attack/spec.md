## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The watched point on the foe's ground
The point a fighter watches on a foe it sees SHALL be the foe's chest across the ground at the height of the ground under the foe; the chase check's "above or below" SHALL measure that point against the fighter's own ground.

#### Scenario: A foe on a rock
- **WHEN** a foe stands on a rock 60 cm higher, 378 cm away
- **THEN** the fighter is not counted stuck for the height
