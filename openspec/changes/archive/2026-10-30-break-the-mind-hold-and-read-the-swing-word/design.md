# Design

The hold break mutates the observer's power list, so `perceive` takes it
mutably. The trust closure the threat estimate calls borrows the same
list, so the brain resolves each third party's trust first; the original
also reads those trusts before its mind check within one pass, so the
order is the original's.

The swing word is `exa_combat::swing::SwingState::flags`, the original's
attack state word: `CLEAVING` (4) is the bit the arc publisher reads as
the recovery and `CONTINUATION | CONTINUED` (0x30000) the score's
commitment mask. The foe's word is read from its fighter, the entity
round 40 made the foe key; the published blow's armed test, the
opponent's reach and its personality skill follow it too, since a
character root carries no blades or brain.

`Spot::level` is the contact's engagement level the combat search passes
per place; the duellist fights at level 3. The removed footwork reduction
had no caller once the seventeen-place search ran.

The arc sine is the one in `exa_physics::engine_math`, which matches the
engine's unordered compare; `exa_ai::combatant::asin_fast` re-exports it
for its existing callers.
