# Proposal

## Why

Phase 7 (Game modes and content) carries the thaumaturgy Force and
Displacement unit routines (SG_Thaum_Force 60%, SG_Thaum_DIsp 57%). A
DeepSeek lane (thaumforce2) made every focus and AI-advice comparison pass
a not-a-number, recorded the material routines' node transitions and the
`gi_multipoint` table, took the creators' sound pitches in double, recorded
each active creator's power object, and stopped the summoning's own node.
The review checked each rule against the original's instructions and found
the release callbacks modelled per material (and as freeing the shaders),
the shutdown's handle count one short, a failed GI entry counted as freed,
the long door's copied vector typed as a rotation, the constant
displacement's cost dropped, the end routines put on the shell instead of
the tendril image, and the constant start given invented shared and pitch
arguments.

## What Changes

- Focus and advice checks refuse only on their ordered branch; a
  not-a-number passes (pace and clip rate go to their refused/"not below"
  side as the instructions do).
- The creators' pitches widen the draw and take 0.4/0.2 and the offset in
  double, narrowed once.
- Each material unit registers one release callback once (Force: force,
  barrier, aura; Displacement: tendril, shell); the callbacks free program
  handles (twelve plus the GI table, or two), never the material shaders.
- A failed GI entry frees nothing at shutdown.
- The active power records: ids, state word 1, costs (the constant
  displacement's included), the long door's tick drain, destination and
  the caster's forward axis (unset on the AI cast), node rules.
- The constant displacement's restore tick ends its tendril image (strand
  records marked 0x8, fade zero, event 0x10); its start asks for an
  unshared shell and plays the hum silent at pitch 1.

## Evidence

The eleven Force and four Displacement casts, the body and displacement
gates, the seven AI evaluators, the shell, kinesis, throw, strikes, vanish,
long door, constant and summon creators, the five material routines, the
two release callbacks, the GI table routine, the two image end routines and
their callers, and the constant displacement's start, read in the
instruction listing; the class of the ended child read from its type
record; constants (0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 0.2, 1.0, 90000, 360000, 160) read from
the executable.

## Out of scope

GPU submission of the effect passes, sound playback, the effect geometry
classes, the summoning's demon, the AI long door's navigation place, and
wiring `ConstantVeil` into a running body.
