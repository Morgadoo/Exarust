## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Companion rise in the AI
A companion's skill rise SHALL write its risen combat skill and confidence trait, each over 100, into its AI's skill share and confidence; the shares the AI derived from them when it was made SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: An armour rise
- **WHEN** a party companion's armour skill rises
- **THEN** its AI's skill share is its combat skill over 100 and its confidence its confidence trait over 100
