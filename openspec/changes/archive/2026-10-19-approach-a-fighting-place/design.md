# Design

`approach_term` is the smoothstep toward the strike ideal. A wide observer uses a 150 cm band and eases the inside fraction. `score_place` applies it only in a fight, then a recovering blow takes `max(close − 1, 0)` and multiplies the approach by `(skill × 2 + 2) × 2`. Outside a fight the approach is cleared.

`publish_arc` wraps the heading plus the degree offsets. A hand is the part point times its 12-float frame, minus the body origin, then the yaw of the flat offset. At phase 0.4 or later that yaw replaces one edge. The look of a side swing is the midpoint of the edges.

`TCombatant` moves from 11% to 22%. The positioning row moves from 60% to 75%. The arc publisher is part of that 22% and is not its own row. The facing scale and the idle-action clock are not in. The AI section stays 1%. The headline stays 14.4% and 5.7%.
