# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Spirit body hovers
The spirit body SHALL be physical: its race's hover flag SHALL hold it up with no legs and the player's walk SHALL move it; its combat mode SHALL stay off.

#### Scenario: Walking the spirit
- **WHEN** the player casts Spirit Form in the arena and holds S, A, W and D in turn
- **THEN** the spirit floats with its pelvis about 92 cm up and travels each way, and Tab leaves it out of combat mode
