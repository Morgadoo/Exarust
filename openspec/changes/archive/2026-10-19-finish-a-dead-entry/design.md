# Design

`knock_out_dead` still returns immediately unless the body is dead, and it still rewrites parts before the tail. The tail runs only then.

The race setup runs when both pair words are 0. Class 0x800 is firm, 0x600 and 0x700 are open, and 0x400 and 0x500 are firm and marked. The body kind then replaces the second hand: 0x100 is open, 0x20100 is firm and marked, and any other kind is none. The bare mark stays on only when the hands are not marked and the body is not large. The class word becomes 0 and the primary becomes -1.

Empty slots up to the hand count are filled. An empty pair fills both extras. The facing is the offset from one point to the other, normalised, with the height cleared, then normalised again. The step reach is the base times (1 − 0.3 × feral) when feral is not negative, times (1 − 0.2 × encumbrance), times 80.

The characters section stays 27%. The headline stays 14.4% and 5.7%. The virtual call and the weapon pass are not in.
