# Design

`knock_out_dead` returns before any write unless the condition is dead. It then clears the marker, stores loose times factor, rewrites the parts and the links, sets posed, stores the step base times one half, sets the behaviour mark, and calls the shipped knock-out with no delay.

A held part's mark gains 0x2000. Its kept span is the span times 1. Its turn is the basis converted to a quaternion: the positive-trace form, or the axis form when the trace is not positive. Every part keeps only gate bits other than 0 and 3. A counted link becomes kind 4 and bits 0x30.

The characters section stays 27%. The headline stays 14.4% and 5.7%. The race setup and the three later calls are not in.
