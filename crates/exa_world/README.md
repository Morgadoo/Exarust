# `exa_world`

Levels in the Bevy world.

**Phase:** 2. Status: props, level scene nodes and lights spawn
(`spawn_level`, `spawn_model`); walls and floors from the tile grid, terrain
and the grid's rooms; portal clipping is next (see
[`docs/PLAN.md`](../../docs/PLAN.md)).

- `spawn_model(commands, model, placement)`: a cached model with its node hierarchy, its lights, fires and voxel lights (`exa_render::gi::VoxelLight`) and its nodes' keyframe tracks (`exa_anim::tracks::NodeTracks`); a placement replaces the root transform, as level props do. Placed items dim their voxel lights and glow meshes by their condition byte. `spawn_model_nodes` also returns the node entities.
- `bodies`: which rigid bodies of a level register in the solver, as the original registers them (each body tree from its top body down through its body children), and their registration: the free trees of props not placed static (`Prop::STATIC`; nodes marked static, `rfc::Node::STATIC`, never register), of placed objects that are no door, lever or push surface, and of the level's own model, asleep; trees hung from the world and mechanisms stay static until their changes land (`LevelInfo::bodies` counts them).
- `mechanisms`: the door and operative records a placed object carries (`MechanismRecords`, under its tagged placed id), converted for `exa_physics`'s door, lever, plate and signal rules; a signal can be dispatched by them. Nothing ticks a mechanism yet: its body hangs from the world by links the solver does not hold.
- `spawn_level(commands, loader, name)`: the level's own meshes and omni lights plus every prop from `Objlib.rpk`.
- `LevelLoad`: the same level built a step at a time within a time budget per step (grid, terrain, level model, props, placed objects, lights), for a loading screen.
- `PlacedCharacter`: a level's placed character resolved for spawning (position, facing, dead or alive, race, worn and held items) with its whole record, from which play mode derives its stats, and the encumbrance its worn items and held shield give it (the level's or the game's items).
- `rooms`: the grid's rooms and door links (the original's flood fill), the cell a point stands in (`cell_of`, and `cell_of_node` clamped to the grid as nodes are sorted), and `RoomTracker`, the original's active, adjacent and hidden rooms around an anchor with their 1 s fades.
- `scene_nodes`: the original's scene-node unit as a pure model, not yet on the draw path (`room_view` still draws from the room states). The scene is its own first sector; nodes outside every scene hang from `_World` (owner `None`). Registration by kind (meshes, lights unless world lights, auxiliary nodes per sector; geometry per scene; the owner always; `IN_SCENE` only for the listed kinds), batch insertion (leading sectors appended, the first other entry registers the whole batch in its owner), locked sectors, change bits and the membership rebuild, subtree moves (local position plus the old origin less the new), child validation (the last child fills an empty slot), frames in sector space, a sector's two mesh boxes (seeded at the first split mesh, the second carrying on from the first, extents capped at each radius, the union radius about the world centre) and the scene's bounds written into its own sector, the 6 m cell and spill mask (one byte, `1/600` multiplier, −1 cm/601 cm; the cell handler runs when the cell changes), and the per-frame visibility: the scene sector's full mask, active sectors against the main views, adjacent sectors through their doorways to active sectors (clipped by the first main view, the scissor from the faces' first vertices rounded ties-to-even), the hidden pass (no view bit and no such doorway), the 20 cm doorway test against the sector beyond, the inherited frame bits and the cut-away fade (0.003 per ms; 0.001 and 0.999 in double precision). It shares [`scene_nodes::cell_spill`] with `light_flood`.
- `light_flood`: a directional light fills `width²` cells with an empty window (cells that list it already keep theirs); the mesh cell and spill test is the scene-node unit's `cell_spill`.
- `room_view`: the rooms in the Bevy world: `LevelRooms`, `RoomAnchor` (the player, or 20 m ahead of the fly camera), `InRoom` on meshes, rooms of props and characters, and the rooms' black shells (`RoomShell`: occluder, lid, fade overlay) shown by state; `RoomView { all }` shows every room.
- `grid`: walls and floors from the wall and floor sets, and each room's occluder and lid merged from the pieces' `[…` and `+R…` strips and the ceiling slots (never spawned as pieces); ceiling caps. Special tile masks turn with the cell, a cell past the last piece of its type reuses it (and one with none of its type takes the set's first), a floor set named `Terrain` is not loaded, and pieces and characters look their room up by the cell they stand in.
- `scene_env`: the original's scene environment as a pure model: the sun and moon on their 45° path and the daylight factor, the time-of-day records and their pick-and-blend, cloud dimming, the fog's span and per-frame near/far, the clear colour and sky gradient, the ambient scale, the automatic sunlight rule, the sun mode and sky switch, and rain and lightning with the original's generator. `environment` turns it into the level's `Lighting` (ambient, sun, linear fog, clear colour and sky) and Bevy resources; the weather tick and the time of day are modelled but nothing drives them yet.
- `terrain`: tiles, blend rules, height pass and plants; in a grid level only the 6 m blocks the grid flags, one mesh per block room.
- `gi_volume`: the voxel GI occupancy block, built on the async compute pool around the camera's focus; each grid cell's floor meshes are merged and get the original's floor heightfield points (`exa_physics::voxel`) once per level, in the cell's own space, and `GiState` tells a loading screen where it stands.
- `point_light(light)`: the original omni light approximated as a Bevy `PointLight`.
- `particles`: the original's CPU particle emitter (`TParticleSystem`): its template record and size scaling, derived per-tick values, ring of Verlet particles, ageing (the fire's distance cap), emission with its two-thirds accumulator, light and sound walks, quad building (diamond, stretched, billboard) with the atlas variants and colour keys, and body contacts. Plain state and rules at the 83 Hz tick; no Bevy `App` needed for a test.
- `fire`: the fire presets: the six templates and three beds, the size tiers (small, medium-small, medium and large at half size), `CreateFire`'s light (including the ×1.25 radius above size 0.2 in double precision), the ring sources, burning items' sizes and the marker import.
- `flame`: the fire emitters in the Bevy world: their fixed ticks, the fire's light density and softness, and each frame's particle mesh with the `_FirePrt` material.
- `arc`: electric arcs in the Bevy world, over `exa_render::electricity`'s rules: the jittered tendrils' camera-facing ribbon with the `_ElecMat01` material and the preset's glow light.
- `nav`: the original's navigation grid (30 cm cells, two layers, cost bytes), built for each level when its load ends from its terrain (the 6 m blocks the level builds), its meshes' GI sample points and its tile-grid floors, on the async compute pool (`NavGrid` resource, `NavState`); its queries (`nav::query`: the line walk, the straight walk, the obstacle test; `nav::walkable`: a point's cost and the nearest walkable point by the original's flood); `--debug-nav`/F6 draws it, `nav::headless` builds it without a window ([`docs/NAVIGATION.md`](../../docs/NAVIGATION.md)).
